# 28 of the 36 sizes fall. Worst +0.0532, best -0.5241. The dictionary at the default
# block is sampled identically either way -- the two strides coincide at 256 -- so every
# published `words` number at 256 is unchanged, and 32 / 64 move 3.245 / 3.045 to 3.235 / 3.041.

# Rejected on the way. Depth, at a fixed budget: runs of 32 / 64 / 128 / 256 / 1024 keys all
# fix `paths` at 1024 (-0.51 to -0.58 B/key) and all sit within +-0.06 elsewhere -- even a run
# of 1024 on a block of 1024, one stride apart from the shipped rule, moves `paths` by -0.31,
# which is the finding underneath the item: with 19 neighbourhoods the table is a lottery.
# Budget, at a run of 32: 50 k / 100 k / 200 k pieces buy `urls` another 0.10 and recover
# `words`, and cost build time in proportion -- 105 -> 198 ms a million keys at 100 k, because
# a piece from `paths` is long. The same size is available for nothing.
