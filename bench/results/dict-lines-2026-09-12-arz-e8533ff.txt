# Rejected. Every `id` gain at 10 M is the size of the control's own drift in the same cell, and
# `key_into` -- which pays the sample search without the in-block data fetch, so it should show
# the effect most clearly -- is slower on the new side in four cells of five. The mechanism the
# line counts predict: 12.5 lines of samples at 10 M is 312 KB, an L2 resident, so a summary
# level removes no cold miss; it only adds accesses, two searches where there was one. Halving
# the samples touched needs a layout that reads a whole cache line per level, not two binary
# searches over the same array.
